## Two-pass review

1. **Source support.** Release identity and tag object were read from the GitHub API; every PR cited as in v0.5.23 appears in the release body. `dsa_dcp.py` hashes were compared at both tags. Cookbook `verified` flags were read cell by cell at `297e54f`. #47935 and `61a3c7f2` were checked with the compare API and are absent from `297e54f`. November 6 follow-up (10:01–10:05 UTC): the `v0.5.23` ref resolves to tag object `7df5dd09…` (the `v0.5.22` ref to `35ce3132…`); Docker Hub tag metadata was read for `v0.5.23`, `latest`, `dev-dsv41` and `dev-muse-glimmer`; PR bodies for #44403, #45107 and #46263 (performance scope), #44901, #44904, #45289 and #45386 (ModelOpt/routing scope), #46424 and #44685 were read; the `quantization_config` of each NVFP4 export was read at its pinned revision, and the hybrid FP8+NVFP4 detection in `configs/model_config.py` and `model_loader/loader.py` was read at `297e54f`; Muse Glimmer support ancestry was rechecked with the compare API at v0.5.17, v0.5.18, v0.5.20 and `297e54f`.
2. **Challenge.** The V4.1 gains in the release notes were measured on 4×GB300 with `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`, not the 1017 checkpoint, and on different layouts: about 28% lower TTFT at 32K/64K input with TP4/EP4, attention DP 1 (#44403); +27% P90 interactivity at TP4, concurrency 4 (#46263); the "up to 1.5×" saturated prefill is the DP4-attention + TP-MoE eager arm (+50%) of #45107, whose TP4 breakable-graph arm gained +37%. Transfer to B300, to the 1017 checkpoint or to 8 GPUs is unverified. [S0523-PR44403](https://api.github.com/repos/sgl-project/sglang/pulls/44403), [S0523-PR46263](https://api.github.com/repos/sgl-project/sglang/pulls/46263), [S0523-PR45107](https://api.github.com/repos/sgl-project/sglang/pulls/45107). Cookbook "verified" means a recorded upstream run, not a run on the atlas node. The image's actual `sglang-kernel` version and the B300 behaviour of v0.5.23 with 1017 remain open.
