## Interpretation

**FP8 export.** 1,046,030,057,472 elements are FP8 E4M3 (99.3%); 7,131,495,712 elements are BF16 (embeddings, head, vision, router, MLA `wkv_a`/`wkv_b`, indexer `wk`/`weights_proj`, norms, DSpark draft); 63,844,608 F32 elements are block scales. `params.json` declares `qscheme_weight: BLOCK`, 128×128 blocks, dynamic BLOCK activations and `use_ue8m0_for_qscales: true`; `config.json` declares `quant_method: fp8`, `activation_scheme: dynamic`, `weight_block_size: [128, 128]`. This is the block-FP8 contract used by DeepSeek-style kernels, not the static per-tensor FP8 of Medium 3.5; do not reuse Medium 3.5 FP8 loader assumptions. A root `dtype: bfloat16` describes non-quantized tensors and compute, not the serialized expert weights. [mistral-large-4-CFG](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B/raw/af26e7869af601441d5cdf75a1272469ddc3d059/config.json) [mistral-large-4-PARAMS](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B/raw/af26e7869af601441d5cdf75a1272469ddc3d059/params.json) [mistral-large-4-HEADERS](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B/tree/af26e7869af601441d5cdf75a1272469ddc3d059)

**NVFP4 export.** Header census: routed experts are packed U8 (514,590,769,152 bytes = two FP4 values per byte for the same 1,029,181,538,304 expert parameters), with 64,323,846,144 FP8 E4M3 group scales (one per 16 values) and per-matrix F32 global scales. Every non-routed tensor has the same dtype and shape as the FP8 export. `params.json` is identical to the FP8 export's except that `quantization` is replaced by a ModelOpt `quantization_config` (71 NVFP4 expert groups; 72 × `wq`, `wo`, FFN `w1/w2/w3` and 44 × indexer `wq_b` as `FP8_PB_WO`). Despite the `WO` suffix, both engines execute `FP8_PB_WO` as W8A8: vLLM maps it to a 128×128 static block weight with dynamic 1×128 activation quantization, and SGLang maps it to `Fp8LinearMethod` with `weight_block_size [128, 128]` and `activation_scheme dynamic` (source inspection, 2026-11-06; see [NVFP4 dossier](nvfp4.md#2-precision-contract-and-how-each-engine-loads-it)). Both engines detect the export as `modelopt_mixed`; routed experts run as NVFP4 W4A4. Whether the FP8 bytes equal the FP8 export's bytes was not established. [mistral-large-4-VLLM-PR-MODELOPT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/28a4495de85c64050b3aad39e946d02791710579/vllm/model_executor/layers/quantization/modelopt.py) [mistral-large-4-SGL-PR-MODELOPT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sgl-project/sglang/eb80b0b66de7d5dda991758a71d8579ae0700526/python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/modelopt_quant.py) The publisher card targets Blackwell and claims "no accuracy regressions" against FP8 from its own evaluation figure (eight tasks; NVFP4 lower on four, error bars overlapping; hardware, engine and sample counts not stated); that is a publisher claim, not reproduced. Scores are transcribed in the [NVFP4 dossier](nvfp4.md#9-quality-evidence-for-the-nvfp4-export). **KV cache precision.** `params.json` sets `dsa.fp8_kv_cache: true` and `fp8_indexer: true`. The NVFP4 card's vLLM command passes `--kv-cache-dtype fp8_ds_mla` as "needed for this checkpoint"; the vLLM recipe states FP8 latent KV (`fp8_ds_mla`) is the default; the SGLang cookbook states FP8 E4M3 KV is the default on Blackwell and BF16 halves capacity (1.34M vs 2.49M tokens on 8×B300). FP8 KV is therefore the publisher/engine default for this model, not an atlas capacity probe — the opposite of Medium 3.5. BF16 KV remains a quality-control comparison.

**Weights by the numbers.** FP8: 1,060,548,427,328 index bytes (+6,160,584 header bytes — 6,160,152 JSON header bytes plus 54 × 8-byte length prefixes — = 1,060,554,587,912 file bytes). NVFP4: 610,030,457,408 index bytes. The 57.5% ratio comes almost entirely from routed experts (1,029.18 GB → 578.91 GB including group scales). ## Other exports observed (not catalog variants)

No NVIDIA-, RedHat- or Inferact-published Mistral Large 4 export was found on 2026-11-06 (Hub searches by author). Observed third-party repositories — `amd/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-Quark-MXFP4` (ROCm-only per the vLLM recipe), `unsloth/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-FP8`, `unsloth/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-NVFP4`, `nota-ai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-NVFP4-Global-Pruned-20`, GGUF/MLX conversions — were not pinned or inspected and carry no atlas support. No separate official EAGLE/draft repository exists; the DSpark draft is embedded in both official checkpoints.

## Deployment rule

Pin model revision, `tekken.json`/chat template, engine image digest and the selected KV dtype together. The DSpark draft is part of the target snapshot, so draft and target cannot drift apart, but disabling it does not remove its 4.2 GB from the download. Compare target-only quality before enabling DSpark, and compare NVFP4 against FP8 on the target workload before adopting NVFP4. [Precision analysis](../../../reports/precision-and-kernels.md) distinguishes NVFP4, MXFP4, FP8 and BF16. [NV4](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/introducing-nvfp4-for-efficient-and-accurate-low-precision-inference/)
