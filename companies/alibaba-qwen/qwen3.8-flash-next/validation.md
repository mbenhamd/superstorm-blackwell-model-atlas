## Decision and prerequisites

**Proposed campaign; no GPU results.** Determine whether the NVIDIA mixed export can deliver useful whole-node capacity while preserving sparse retrieval, recurrent state, n-gram lookups and multimodal quality. Its 125B main-model headline omits the separately reported 51B n-gram table and 4B MTP component. The 36 GDN layers and 12 QSA layers need separate state/history accounting; a 2,048-token sparse selection budget is not a cache or context cap. [Architecture](architecture.md), [qwen3.8-flash-next-CARD](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next/raw/de4b8e4d43b917e7706784d8bb445c9af86a3540/README.md).

Planning evidence was refreshed on 2026-10-15 UTC (October 16 in Paris). Current publisher card/config bytes match the pinned base revision; the current base, official FP8 and NVIDIA repository revisions match [model.json](model.json). Use those identities in a separate [experiment manifest](../../../benchmarks/experiment.template.json) for every arm. A recipe or successful target-only load does not certify all mixed tensors, MTP or B300 performance.

## Quality workloads and why they belong

Apply the [shared benchmark selection and evaluation rules](../../../benchmarks/MODEL-BENCHMARKS.md), including frozen evaluation contracts, paired uncertainty and an answer-retaining scorer. Use its EvalPlus functional screen and a frozen LiveCodeBench release/date range before an expensive repository-agent campaign. Report pass@1, invalid programs and evaluator failures under the same language/runtime, attempt policy and budgets for each export and thinking mode.

| Workload | Proposed evaluation and rationale | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QSA/GDN long history | Pin RULER v1 and its NeMo-Skills dependency; use fixed retrieval, variable-tracking and aggregation payloads at increasing lengths. Place required evidence at different positions and add held-out application questions spanning several documents. | Score each task/length. Synthetic retrieval does not prove reasoning over real documents or the advertised extended context. Pin the current v1 pipeline rather than the deprecated main-branch runner. [MBENCH-RULER-V1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA/RULER/rulerv1-ns/README.md), [MBENCH-RULER](https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER). |
| Instructions and tool arguments | Run pinned IFEval with prompt-level strict accuracy; add English/Chinese structured requests, identifiers, escaped strings, multi-turn tool results and known expected arguments. These test export quality and errors that table-heavy or multilingual traffic may expose. | Keep validity and semantic correctness separate. Public-benchmark results complement a held-out application set and do not establish a causal n-gram effect. [MBENCH-IFEVAL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-research/google-research/master/instruction_following_eval/README.md). |
| Image understanding | Use a fixed single-document DocVQA validation set with official ANLS scoring, plus distinct local multi-image/follow-up regressions. Freeze images, processor, resize settings and answer extraction. | Report quality, visual-token counts, encoder/preprocessor time and wrong-image answers. Single-image accuracy does not qualify video; a video campaign must freeze sampled frames/timestamps and labels separately. [MBENCH-DOCVQA](https://www.docvqa.org/datasets/docvqa), [MBENCH-DOCVQA-EVAL](https://rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=17&com=tasks). |
| Coding deployment, if required | Use SWE-bench Verified with a pinned agent harness, task images, tool rules and token/turn/time budgets, followed by an untouched evaluation set after tuning. | Report resolved tasks and full task wall time, including tool latency. This is independent application evaluation, not reproduction of the NVIDIA or Qwen coding scores. [MBENCH-SWEBENCH](https://www.swebench.com/SWE-bench/). |

Keep thinking and non-thinking as separate workload strata. The publisher documents `enable_thinking`, `preserve_thinking`, `reasoning_effort` and different sampling settings for the two modes; the SGLang cookbook's contrary prose is stale. Save the serialized conversation so preserved reasoning changes cannot masquerade as cache or engine gains. Measure first final answer as well as first reasoning content. [qwen3.8-flash-next-CARD](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next/raw/de4b8e4d43b917e7706784d8bb445c9af86a3540/README.md).

NVIDIA's paired FP8/NVFP4 table is exporter evidence, not a local quality gate. Its declared `max_new_tokens=131072` and `reasoning_effort=xhigh` do not fit the current 65,536-token profile as a reproduction setup. Retain its numbers as attributed results; any attempted reproduction needs the original evaluation protocol, adequate context/output budgets and a separately qualified capacity configuration. [qwen3.8-flash-next-NV](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4/raw/fc694b54fb0174e0913e6adf86691ef85a4ead47/README.md).

## Ordered experiments

| ID | Controlled comparison and mechanism | Measurements and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QFN-01: mixed load | Qualify each stored target-only profile on stable and one pinned candidate build. Audit stored main/PLE/MTP/vision components separately from resident target tensors and documented loader exclusions; record serialized bytes versus host/HBM residency, repacking and transient peaks. | A TP1 recipe passes only if all required target components, including PLE and the enabled vision path, plus the desired working set fit with the declared reserve. Deliberate target-only MTP omission is valid and must be inventoried. Reject missing required tensors, unplanned dtype expansion or unexplained fallback; qualify MTP residency in QFN-05. |
| QFN-02: export quality | On a qualified common engine/build and TP/EP layout, compare official FP8 with NVIDIA NVFP4, keeping sparse KV, GDN state, table placement, sampling and processor fixed. Add BF16 only after separate qualification. | Produce paired quality deltas and task failures, plus actual load/steady-state memory. If no common supported layout/dtype exists, report a complete configuration comparison and do not attribute the result to weight precision alone. |
| QFN-03: n-gram placement | Only where the exact export/backend supports it, compare GPU-resident PLE with the documented host-offload path. Hold table contents/precision, NUMA placement, CPU allocation and all serving controls fixed. Replay both repeated local-token-pattern and diverse identifier/multilingual cohorts with cold prefix caches. | Measure actual table/scale bytes, host pinned memory, transfer bandwidth, lookup stalls and p95/p99 latency under concurrency. Keep each cohort identical across arms. A synthetic locality win does not predict real traffic; removing PLE would change the model. |
| QFN-04: GDN/QSA state | Compare identical logical requests from fresh prefill, warm prefixes, divergent suffixes, eviction and cancellation, interleaving unrelated sessions. Shift evidence across four-token QSA micro-block neighborhoods and actual backend chunk/cache boundaries; include histories exceeding the selection budget. | Report retrieval/task scores by length/position, state and sparse-cache pools, reuse counters and continuation errors. Preserve recurrent/convolution state as well as selected token history. Qualify any supported BF16/FP32 state change as its own quality experiment. |
| QFN-05: mixed MTP | First create a qualified TP8+EP, autotuning-disabled, speculation-off control matching the [MTP profile](../../../profiles/qwen3.8-flash-next.nvfp4.vllm.mtp.json). Qualify the speculative arm's MTP tensors/scales and FP8 precision, then compare its one-token MTP arm; deeper proposals follow shape and quality checks. | Confirm FP8 MTP execution, record natural acceptance, verification/draft work, state rollback, stop handling and memory. Measure both low load and sustained traffic. The stored plain-TP8 target-only arm does not isolate MTP because it also differs in EP and autotuning. |
| QFN-06: whole node | After individual qualification, compare one vLLM TP8 group with a measured, disjoint SGLang TP1 replica count up to the eight-GPU budget. TP4/EP or other replica layouts require new manifests and load gates. Match offered traffic and include routing/cache locality. | Report consumed GPUs, whole-node goodput, first-answer tails, CPU/storage pressure and memory per replica. Do not multiply a TP1 result by eight or call TP1 versus TP8 an equal-resource engine result. Use the same tuning budget and report remaining dtype/layout differences. |

The inspected vLLM target loader explicitly filters `mtp.` weights. Its target-only resident inventory can therefore be smaller than the stored checkpoint without losing a required target component. Check the chosen image's registered path and account for added MTP memory when enabling speculation. [MBENCH-QFN-TARGET-LOADER](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/f8460ef23659afd69046a81529ec2295e4c3f686/vllm/models/qwen4_exp/nvidia/model.py).

Use complete serialized input buckets of 4,096, 16,384, 32,768 and 61,440 tokens with 128/1,024 output caps, plus a 4,096-input/8,192-output generation trace. Count image/template/reasoning tokens within the current 65,536 cap and report achieved lengths and truncations. Begin at concurrency 1 and 8, then 32 and a fixed offered-rate ladder; include short chat mixed with long document/image prefill. Separate cold and repeated-prefix runs. Quality evaluations receive a budget sufficient for final answers rather than inheriting short shape-probe caps.

Extension toward native 262,144 context and any YaRN path toward 1M are separate experiments. Pin the positional-scaling configuration, retest short as well as long prompts, and establish load/state capacity before evaluating accuracy. An extended-context publisher claim is not a result for these profiles.

## Required outputs and promotion

Deliver a complete manifest, quantization/tensor-placement audit, raw request and response records, quality scores by mode/task/modality/length, table/state/KV memory, profiler evidence for lookup or communication claims, and a paired decision table. Follow the [protocol](../../../benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md) for repeated randomized comparisons, uncertainty, offered load and failures. The [client result schema](../../../benchmarks/result.schema.json) does not replace quality or tensor-placement evidence.

Declare quality/regression thresholds, latency/error budgets, memory reserve and minimum useful improvement before running. Reject missing required target or enabled-draft tensors, corrupted continuation, failed quality gates or inability to sustain the stated workload. A faster result with inadequate uncertainty or missing effective dtype/placement evidence is inconclusive. Preserve failed and unmeasured arms explicitly; no B300 winner or production SLA is implied.
