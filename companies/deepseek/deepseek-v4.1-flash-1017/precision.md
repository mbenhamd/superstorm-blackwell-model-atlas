Reviewed 2026-11-06 (UTC). Native pin `1bae0e1088993b9b82de08f59de5be5a34e42170`; NVIDIA export pin `3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1`. Dtypes below come from the 48 pinned safetensors headers and the configurations; no checkpoint was loaded.

| native | `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017` @ `1bae0e108899` | Routed and DSpark experts MXFP4 (packed E2M1 in I8, one E8M0 scale per 32 inputs); attention, shared experts, indexer query and Engram projections FP8 E4M3 with E8M0 scales per 32×32 block; Engram tables FP8 E4M3 with E8M0 per 32 values; embedding, LM head, gates, compressors, norms, ViT and aligner BF16; mHC parameters and routing biases FP32 |

## Interpretation

`quant_method: fp8` with `expert_dtype: fp4` describes a mixed checkpoint, not an all-FP8 model; the Hugging Face "fp8/8-bit" tags do not describe the FP4 experts. Compared with 0731, the FP8 block is now 32×32 instead of 128×128 (`weight_block_size` and the reference `fp8_block_size = 32`), and the FP4 expert format is unchanged in kind (E2M1 with E8M0 per 32). Kernels must support the 32-wide UE8M0 FP8 blocks; SGLang's cookbook warns that overriding the auto-selected backends sends these blocks to a Triton fallback that dominates decode. [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-CFG](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017/raw/1bae0e1088993b9b82de08f59de5be5a34e42170/config.json) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-INFER-SRC](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017/blob/1bae0e1088993b9b82de08f59de5be5a34e42170/inference/model.py) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-SGL-COOKBOOK-MAIN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sgl-project/sglang/80eedfaf2fb34e392eaacdbb78a7cff92d6b647d/docs/cookbook/autoregressive/DeepSeek/DeepSeek-V4_1.mdx)

**Cache precision is part of the model contract.** The reference quantizes window KV to FP8 (block 32), compressed KV latents to FP4 with E4M3 scales per 16 (the card's "FP4 main KV caching"), and indexer queries/keys to FP4 with E8M0 per 32. The publisher's 890 bytes/token assumes that FP4 compressed cache ([derivation](architecture.md#memory-and-performance-consequences)). In vLLM v0.32.0, `FLASHMLA_MEGA_ATTN_DSV41` accepts `nvfp4_ds_mla` ("V4.1 fp8 SWA cache + NVFP4 compressed cache") and resolves an unspecific `--kv-cache-dtype` to it, while `fp8` is an alias for `fp8_ds_mla`; `FLASHINFER_MLA_SPARSE_DSV41` on SM10x accepts only `auto`/`bfloat16`/`fp8`/`fp8_e4m3` and uses a per-tensor FP8 layout. The vLLM recipe sets `--kv-cache-dtype fp8` on Blackwell for both backends, so the recipe does not store the publisher's FP4 compressed format. Whether that improves or degrades quality relative to the trained FP4 cache is unmeasured. [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-VLLM032-SPARSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/5df63a7203cdb9328d824a27b682e77c7b9928be/vllm/models/deepseek_v41/sparse_mla.py) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-VLLM032-MEGA](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/5df63a7203cdb9328d824a27b682e77c7b9928be/vllm/models/deepseek_v41/nvidia/flash_mla_mega_attn.py) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-VLLM032-FI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/5df63a7203cdb9328d824a27b682e77c7b9928be/vllm/models/deepseek_v41/nvidia/flashinfer_sparse.py)

`indexer_kv_dtype: mxfp4` in the vLLM recipe corresponds to the reference's FP4 indexer keys with E8M0 per 32; it is a separate setting from `--kv-cache-dtype`. [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-VR](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/recipes/951d01b445c619868d9cdf8903ccbe2bc960cf0b/models/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash.yaml)

## The NVIDIA export is a separate qualification

NVIDIA states the source revision as `1bae0e1088993b9b82de08f59de5be5a34e42170`, Model Optimizer v0.48.0rc0, calibration on 1,024 samples of length 512, and GB300 test hardware. Its `text_config` and `vision_config` are identical to the native config; only `quantization_config` differs, and its `config.json` and `hf_quant_config.json` are byte-identical to those of `nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-NVFP4` (the predecessor's export). [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV-CARD](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4/raw/3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1/README.md) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV-CFG](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4/raw/3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1/config.json) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV-QCFG](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4/raw/3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1/hf_quant_config.json)

- **Expert weights.** NVIDIA reports all 16,986,931,200 expert weight blocks passed lossless MXFP4→NVFP4 conversion, with 24 of 46,080 projection entries on fallback activation scales. Arithmetic check: 384 × 40 × 3 projections × (5,120 × 2,304) / 32 = 16,986,931,200, so the stated count is the number of source MXFP4 32-value blocks (each becomes two NVFP4 16-value groups), and 384 × 40 × 3 = 46,080 is the projection count. Lossless weight conversion does not make W4A4 activations equivalent to the source's FP8-activation path. (Publisher claim; block count derived.)
- **Engram tables.** Converted FP8 → MXFP4: 136 instead of 264 bytes per 256-value row including scales (≈97.3 GiB versus ≈188.8 GiB for 768,022,850 rows). NVIDIA labels this conversion lossy. (Derived arithmetic; publisher claim of lossiness.)
- **Draft.** `mtp.*` is excluded, so the DSpark stages keep their MXFP4 experts. The NVIDIA vLLM command was validated without speculation; the SGLang command was validated with DSpark (publisher GSM8K 96.82% with DSpark, 96.59% without).
- **Storage.** The 48 LFS shard sizes sum to 428,986,460,424 bytes (≈399.5 GiB), matching NVIDIA's "approximately 400 GiB". The export's `model.safetensors.index.json` still reports `total_size` 510,286,023,000, the source value; treat that metadata field as stale. [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV-TREE](https://huggingface.co/api/models/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4/tree/3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1) [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV-INDEX](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4/resolve/3ae9c0d3fbbea5370bd06e679d22044db15883f1/model.safetensors.index.json)
- **Publisher evaluation.** On vLLM, NVFP4 + MXFP4 Engram versus MXFP4 source: GPQA Diamond 92.93 vs 92.77, AA-LCR 80.19 vs 80.41, SciCode 57.32 vs 57.58, IFBench 76.47 vs 76.33, MMMU-Pro 78.73 vs 78.38, Terminal-Bench 2.1 81.11 vs 81.81 (publisher benchmark, GB300, not reproduced).

**Engine readiness of this export.** vLLM needs open PR 61530 for MXFP4 Engram tables [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-VLLM-PR61530](https://api.github.com/repos/vllm-project/vllm/pulls/61530) (stock images, including v0.32.0, cannot load them per NVIDIA). SGLang needs PR 47935 [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-SGL-PR47935](https://api.github.com/repos/sgl-project/sglang/pulls/47935), merged 2026-11-04 and absent from both v0.5.22 and v0.5.23. Neither atlas baseline release can load this export.

The predecessor's export, `nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-NVFP4`, is based on V4.1 Flash revision `dba1be0a40aa` and is not a 1017 artifact. [deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-NV41-CARD](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-NVFP4/raw/c48dca0146b56d64c8116dbb6fcace8b7cc835f8/README.md)

Other exports found on the Hub (AMD Quark MXFP4/PTPC-FP8 and a RedHatAI duplicate of the V4.1 launch repository) are based on the predecessor and do not bear on B300 serving of 1017; GGUF/EXL3/MLX community conversions are out of scope.

## Deployment rule

Pin model and tokenizer revisions, quantization configuration, engine commit and image digest together. Record the resolved attention backend, `--kv-cache-dtype`, `indexer_kv_dtype`, MoE backend and Engram placement for each run, because one flag name maps to different cache layouts per backend. Compare target-only quality before changing cache precision or enabling DSpark. The [precision analysis](../../../reports/precision-and-kernels.md) explains the formats.
