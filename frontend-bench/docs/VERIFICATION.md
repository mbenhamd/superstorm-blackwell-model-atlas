## Architecture route (8 November 2026)

New route `#/architecture` (sidebar after Models, ⌘K, a link from the TL;DR controls). Three tabs: the layer map (one strip per model, one cell per decoder layer coloured by attention mixer kind, with an active/total parameter bar), Attention & KV, and Experts; a side sheet per model. All values come from the catalog snapshot (`src/data/atlas.json`) and the atlas model reports; nothing is measured and no figure was taken from outside the atlas.

- **Derivation.** `src/domain/architecture.ts` builds the per-layer schedule from publisher configuration fields. Each model states how its order was obtained: recorded (an explicit per-layer list), interval (a full-attention interval), ratio-derived, uniform, or counts-only (counts known, order not). KV bytes per token are calculations from head counts and dimensions at BF16, shown as calculations.
- **Checked by hand against the atlas reports.** KV bytes per token: Qwen 3.8 27B 65,536; Mistral Medium 360,448; GLM 5.3 89,856; GLM Flash 11,264; Qwen Flash Next 24,576; Muse 13,312 (+39,936); Mistral Large 4 71,424 = 54 × 576 × 2 + 9 × 512 × 2. Sparse-index reuse: GLM 5.3 has 21 layers with full index selection and 57 that reuse one (`companies/z-ai/glm-5.3/architecture.md`, `indexer_types`); Mistral Large 4 has 44 `full` and 28 `shared` (`companies/mistral-ai/mistral-large-4/architecture.md`).
- **dashboardcn.** DataTableCard, DataTableColumnHeader, DistributionBar, RadialGauge (active/total parameters, a bounded fraction) and MetricValue with `compactFrom={Infinity}`. The layer strips are custom (no registry item carries a per-layer encoding); they reuse card, tooltip and the tokens.
- **Palette.** Mixer-kind tokens in light and dark (six kinds, plus recessive steps for reused sparse and compressed layers) (`styles.css`), checked with the dataviz validator. Every pair that shares a row passes in both modes. The full six-colour set fails one pair (KDA vs compressed attention, CVD ΔE 3.3 light / 3.5 dark). Those two kinds never occur in the same model, and each cell also carries its kind in the tooltip and the legend.
- **Accessibility.** Each strip has `role="img"` with the layer counts in its `aria-label`. Arrow keys step through a strip's layers. Rows open the sheet with Enter, and focus returns on close. The 390 px layout and dark mode are covered by a browser test.
